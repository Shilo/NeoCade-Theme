## Specific Ideas

- **Per the user's redirect message:** the goal is "colorful, flat and modern UI. Not outdated 3d, texture and engraving." Research must surface MD3 + MD3 Expressive principles that deliver "colorful and playful and expressive" while keeping "flat modern."
- **Material Design 3 and MD3 Expressive** are the primary reference because: (a) most evolved flat-design system; (b) covers color/type/shape/motion/state systematically; (c) has a clean component library that maps to Godot Controls; (d) MD3 Expressive specifically increases personality/saturation, which addresses the user's "colorful + playful" desire without violating "no textures."
- **LDtk's flat discipline** (per Phase 2) is also compatible with this direction — LDtk uses solid-color chrome, no gradients, restrained category colors. LDTK-UI-MINING.md remains a useful loose-inspiration cross-reference.
- **Flat-3D Game UI / Extruded Flat UI** as a *raised* variation, NOT replacing flat — it's an alternative that can ship per-theme. Common mobile-game pattern: pink button on top, darker pink shadow shape underneath (offset down ~4-8px), no soft shadow, no gradient, no texture. Pure shape duplication.
- **The user's reference images** (the two itch.io ones they linked) are the canonical "this is exactly what I mean by extruded flat" references. Phase 3.1 must screenshot or precisely describe these patterns.
- **Phase 3 v0 "Prize Pop Plaza"** was the closest direction the user actually liked — childish, friendly, mobile-game vibe, raised buttons OK. The flat-MD3 + extruded-flat raised treatment is essentially "Prize Pop Plaza but flat MD3, not painterly." Phase 3.1 research should informally validate this.
