Reading the cells

For every row, watch the depth strip. Under current, the depth strip on bright colors (hot pink, gold, royal blue) reads as a different hue family than the button face -- the BRIEF's "muted-purple-black" complaint. Under hsv-darken 40% the depth strip reads as "noticeably darker pink" / "noticeably darker gold" -- same hue, same saturation, just darker. Hue rotation in green/red on the metric line confirms it numerically.

Note also: under current, very dark NeoCade button-normal colors (#1E2640, #1A0F2E) get pulled further toward base + black -- the depth strip is barely distinguishable from the button face. Under hsv-darken 40%, even the dark normal button reads as a perceptibly darker dark, because percentage-based darkening preserves contrast at any luminance.
